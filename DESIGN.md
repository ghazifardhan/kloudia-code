# Design Direction: Kloudia Code

## Identity & Mood
- **Style**: Modern Sleek Minimal
- **Personality**: Focused, precise, uncluttered, professional developer tooling.

## Palette
- **Primary Accent**: `#36A1D7` (Kloudia Cyan)
- **Background**: `#0F1117` (Deep Dark) / `#1A1D27` (Card Surface)
- **Text**: `#E6EDF3` (Primary), `#8B949E` (Muted/Secondary)
- **Border**: `#30363D` (Subtle boundary)

## Typography
- **Headings & Accents**: `Space Grotesk`, sans-serif
- **Code & Terminals**: `JetBrains Mono` / `Geist Mono`, monospace

## Dials
- **ENERGY**: 1 (Calm, disciplined, non-distracting)
- **RHYTHM**: 1 (Structured, uniform, predictable)
- **MOTION**: 1 (Subtle hover & clean state transitions only)
